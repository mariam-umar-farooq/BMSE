The Marketing Plan

Developing a marketing plan means moving through four steps — objectives, segments, positioning, and programs — ending in a concrete set of actions built on the 7 Ps of marketing.

Source: "Segmentation, targeting and positioning for social entrepreneurship" slides by Marta Giovannetti
