A Social Venture Still Needs a Strategy

⏱ About 8 minutes

A social mission is a reason to exist — but it isn't a plan. Social entrepreneurship still needs management and strategy: the means by which an organisation achieves its objectives through the deliberate use of its resources. This module introduces how strategy works, with the sustainable-footwear company Veja as our running example.

Purpose and plan are different things. Where does the one you're thinking of sit?

The Strategic Process — with Veja

⏱ About 18 minutes

Strategy formation follows a process. You set direction, understand your context, then decide how to compete and create value. Here it is, step by step — then you'll apply two parts of it to Veja.

The strategic process
1
Mission, vision & goals — why do we exist? (mission) · what do we want to be? (vision) · goals, shaped by stakeholders
2
Analyse the environment — the general environment (PESTEL) and the sector (competition, entrants, substitutes)
3
Analyse the firm internally — strengths and weaknesses, resources and capabilities (SWOT brings this together with the environment)
4
Formulate the strategy — decide how to create value and build advantage
5
Implement & control — put it into action, then monitor and adjust
💡 Social value creation goes beyond CSR. Reducing harm and being a good corporate citizen is the floor. Creating Shared Value means building measurable value by addressing social problems that intersect with the business itself — the social mission and the strategy are one and the same.
Veja — the running example
Veja makes sustainable trainers, born from two French friends' shared concern for social justice and sustainability. The shoes use ecological raw materials from producers' cooperatives — organic cotton from Peruvian and Brazilian growers, and wild rubber from the Amazon, whose purchase generates resources to help preserve the rainforest. Footwear production is highly polluting (≈1.4% of global greenhouse-gas emissions), and there's growing social concern for the environment and a shift toward repairing rather than replacing shoes.
🌍 PESTEL — spot the forces. The Veja description points to forces in the general environment. Which PESTEL dimensions are most clearly at play, and how?
PESTEL = Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal. At least two are strongly present here.
💚 Shared value. How does Veja create shared value — where does solving a social/environmental problem and running the business become the same act?
Look at the rubber-from-the-Amazon detail. Where does mission = strategy?
✍️ Write a social mission. Draft a one-sentence mission statement for Veja — the "why do we exist?" that captures both the social purpose and the business.
A good social mission names who/what it serves and the change it exists to make.
