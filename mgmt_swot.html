SWOT Analysis

SWOT brings together the two analyses in the strategic process: what's happening inside the firm, and what's happening outside in its environment.

Source: "Introduction to management and strategic management" slides by Desiderio J. García Almeida
Helpful
Harmful
Internal
(the firm)

Strengths

Internal attributes and resources that support success — a loyal community, a strong mission, committed people.

Weaknesses

Internal factors that make success harder — a weak brand, thin funding, gaps in skills.

External
(environment)

Opportunities

External chances to seize — new needs, supportive policy, partnerships, shifting attitudes.

Threats

External risks to prepare for — changing regulations, rising costs, new competitors.

The "S&W" come from analysing the firm internally; the "O&T" come from analysing the environment.
