The 7 Ps of the Marketing Mix

Once you've chosen your target market, the marketing mix is the set of levers you shape to serve it. Every P is aimed at the same centre: the target market.

Source: "Segmentation, targeting and positioning for social entrepreneurship" slides by Marta Giovannetti
1Product
  • Design
  • Technology
  • Usefulness
  • Convenience
  • Value
  • Quality
  • Packaging
  • Branding
  • Accessories
  • Warranties
2Place
  • Retail
  • Wholesale
  • Mail order
  • Internet
  • Direct sales
  • Peer to peer
  • Multi-channel
3Price
Strategies: Skimming, Penetration, Psychological, Cost-Plus, Loss leader, etc.
4Promotion
  • Special offers
  • Advertising
  • Endorsements
  • User trials
  • Direct mailing
  • Leaflets/posters
  • Free gifts
  • Competitions
  • Joint ventures
5Physical Environment
  • Smart
  • Run-down
  • Interface
  • Comfort
  • Facilities
TARGET MARKET
every P serves this centre
6Process
  • Especially relevant to service industries
  • How are services consumed?
7People
  • Employees
  • Management
  • Culture
  • Customer service
For a social venture, "People" and "Process" matter as much as Product and Price — the mission shows in how you treat people and how you deliver.
