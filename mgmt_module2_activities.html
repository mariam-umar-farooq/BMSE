The Business Model on One Page

⏱ About 10 minutes

A business model is the story of how an organisation creates, delivers, and captures value — simple enough to explain in a minute. The Business Model Canvas turns that story into nine building blocks on a single page. The Social Business Model Canvas adds the social mission and impact to the picture.

💡 Model vs. plan: the business model is the core content — the short story. The business plan is the guide to action — the full story. When a model doesn't work, it's usually because the narrative doesn't make sense, or the numbers don't add up. The canvas is a dynamic prototype: v0, v1, v2… you redesign it as you learn.

Module 2 · Activity 2 of 3

Build Your Social Business Model Canvas

⏱ About 30 minutes

Fill each block for your venture. Keep it short — a few words or a line per block. Remember it's a prototype: you can redraw it as you learn. Start with the Value Proposition and Customer Segments (the heart), then work outward.

🎁 Offer
Value Proposition
What value do you deliver? Which problem do you solve, for whom?
👥 Customers
Customer Segments
Who are your customers and beneficiaries? (These can differ.)
Channels
How do you reach and deliver to them?
Customer Relationships
What relationship do you build with each segment?
🏠 Infrastructure
Key Activities
What must you do well to deliver the value proposition?
Key Resources
What assets do you need? (people, materials, tech, funding)
Key Partners
Who helps you? (suppliers, cooperatives, allies)
€ Economic analysis
Cost Structure
What are your main costs?
Revenue Streams
How do you earn income to sustain the venture?
➕ The social layer
Social & Environmental Mission
What social or environmental problem are you here to solve?
Impact Measures
How will you know you're creating real social value?
Surplus
What happens to any surplus? How is it reinvested in the mission?
🎯 Competitor check: if you think you have no competitors, you haven't finished the analysis. Who else meets this need today — even imperfectly? Note them, and what makes your model different.
🔍 Who are your competitors or alternatives — and what makes your model different?
Module 2 · Activity 3 of 3

Reflection

⏱ About 6 minutes

The canvas is a conversation-starter, not a final answer.

🧩 Which block was hardest to fill — and what does that tell you about your idea?
An empty or fuzzy block is a signal, not a failure.
💬 If you showed this canvas to a potential co-founder, what's the first question they'd ask?
The canvas exists to start exactly this kind of conversation.
🔁 What's the one assumption in your model you most need to test next?
A canvas is v0. What would move it to v1?
