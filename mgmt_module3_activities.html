Marketing That Serves a Mission

⏱ About 8 minutes

In social entrepreneurship, marketing isn't about persuasion for its own sake. It starts with a shift: from shareholder and customer value toward stakeholder value — from individual value to collective value, measured in social, environmental, and economic impact. This module uses Veja as its running example.

💡 The value shift: stakeholder value is the positive benefit that employees, customers, suppliers, investors, and communities receive from an organisation's activities. Stakeholder engagement is fundamental to sustainability — it integrates the social with the economic and environmental.
🎯 Think of a product or service you bought partly because of what the company stands for. What made you choose it?
This is stakeholder value at work — you valued more than the product itself.

Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning — with Veja

⏱ About 20 minutes

The STP process is how marketing turns a broad market into a focused plan: divide the market into segments, choose which to target, and position yourself clearly. Then the marketing mix (the 7 Ps) turns that into action.

The STP + planning flow
1
Objectives — set clear, realistic, measurable goals
2
Segmentation — group customers by needs a product/service can satisfy
3
Targeting — profile and select the segments you'll serve
4
Positioning — who you are and how you're different; anchor it in your value proposition
5
Programs (7 Ps) — Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People, Process, Physical evidence
Veja — the running example
Veja makes minimalist white trainers with a distinctive "V", from ecological materials sourced through producers' cooperatives. It famously spends little on conventional advertising, reinvesting instead in fair sourcing and transparency — a positioning built on sustainability and social justice rather than marketing spend.
👥 Segment & target. Which customer segment is Veja really targeting, and what do they value?
Think beyond "people who need shoes" — who chooses Veja specifically, and why?
📍 Positioning. In one sentence, how is Veja positioned — who are they, and how are they different?
A good positioning statement makes the difference obvious.
🎛️ Marketing mix. Pick two of the 7 Ps and describe how Veja handles them differently from a conventional shoe brand.
Promotion and Product are obvious starting points — but any two work.
