How People Learn at Work: 70-20-10

A common model of where workplace learning actually comes from. Most of it happens by doing — a reminder that a social venture develops its people through real experience, not just courses.

Source: "Team management and organizational structure" slides by Alberto Zanutto
70%
Experiences — learning by doing
20%
Peer training — learning from others
10%
Formal training
Knowledge is also tacit (personal, experience-based) as well as explicit (codified in documents) — Nonaka & Takeuchi, 1995.
