Analysing the Environment

Before setting strategy, a venture reads two layers of its environment: the wide general environment, and the specific sector it competes in.

Source: "Introduction to management and strategic management" slides by Desiderio J. García Almeida (PESTEL; Porter's five forces)

PESTEL

the general environment
🏛️
Political — government, stability, policy direction
💶
Economic — growth, costs, purchasing power
👥
Social — attitudes, demographics, values
💡
Technological — innovation, new tools
🌍
Environmental — climate, sustainability, resources
⚖️
Legal — laws, regulations, standards

Porter's Five Forces

the sector or industry
Threat of new entrants — how easily others can enter
Bargaining power of suppliers — how much leverage they hold
Rivalry between established competitors
Bargaining power of buyers — how much leverage customers hold
Threat of substitutes — other ways to meet the same need
