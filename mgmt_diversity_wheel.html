The Diversity Wheel

Each person is a "jigsaw puzzle" of traits — some visible, many not. The wheel sorts them into core primary dimensions and acquired secondary ones.

Source: "Team management and organizational structure" slides by Alberto Zanutto (diversity wheel: Loden & Rosener, 1991)
