Culture Shapes the Workplace

⏱ About 8 minutes

Organisational culture shapes how people work — and who gets to thrive. For a social venture, diversity management and inclusion aren't add-ons: they're part of the mission. This final module looks at how HR practices build inclusive, sustainable environments where people are retained and engaged.

💡 Diversity vs. inclusion: diversity is who is in the room; inclusion is whether they can genuinely participate, be heard, and belong. You can have one without the other — and only the combination changes outcomes.
🌍 Think of a team or group where you truly felt you belonged. What made that possible?
Belonging is built through specific, often small, actions. What were they?
🚧 And the opposite — a time you felt excluded or unheard. What was missing?
Naming the gap is how you learn to design it out.
Module 5 · Activity 2 of 3

Building Inclusion & Wellbeing

⏱ About 16 minutes

Inclusion and wellbeing don't happen by accident — they're built through deliberate HR practices. These are some of the levers that help people feel they belong, and that keep them engaged and retained.

Levers for inclusion & wellbeing
🎯
Diversity management — actively recruiting, developing, and valuing a range of backgrounds, and addressing unconscious bias
🗣️
Voice & participation — structures where people can genuinely be heard and shape decisions
⚖️
Gender sensitivity — attention to how policies and everyday practices affect people differently
🌱
Wellbeing — conditions that support health, balance, and sustainable engagement over time
🔁
Retention & engagement — HR practices that make people want to stay and contribute fully
🧠 Unconscious bias shapes decisions we believe are neutral — in hiring, promotion, and everyday interactions. Naming it is the first step; building checks into processes is what actually reduces its effect.
🛠️ Choose one lever above. What's one concrete practice a small social venture could realistically adopt?
Small and doable beats ambitious and abstract. What could a 5-person team actually do?
🔎 Where might unconscious bias creep into a small venture's hiring or teamwork — and how would you guard against it?
Even mission-driven teams aren't immune. Where's the risk, and what's the check?
