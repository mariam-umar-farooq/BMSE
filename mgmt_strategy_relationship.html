Strategy and its Environment

Strategy is the business's relationship with its environment: the actions it takes to reach its objectives and improve performance through the rational use of resources.

Source: "Introduction to management and strategic management" slides by Desiderio J. García Almeida (framework: Guerras & Navas, 2022)

The environment shapes what's possible; strategy turns resources into competitive advantage; performance creates value — and stakeholders both influence and receive that value. For a social venture, "performance" means social impact, not only profit.
