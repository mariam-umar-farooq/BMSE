Positioning: the Perceptual Map

A perceptual map plots where a brand sits in the market against its competitors, on the dimensions that matter most to customers — often price and quality.

Source: "Segmentation, targeting and positioning for social entrepreneurship" slides by Marta Giovannetti

Perceptual maps represent dimensions that are relevant for the business positioning and allow to visualise the relative position occupied in the market by the brand and the competitors.

Perceptual maps can be two-dimensional graphs that compare two criteria, often price and quality (two relevant dimensions of value), to position their brands towards the competitors.
