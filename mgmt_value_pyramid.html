Social impact — self-transcendence: helping others or society beyond oneself.
Life changing — value that reshapes a person's life: hope, belonging, motivation.
Emotional — how it makes people feel: reduces anxiety, wellness, design.
Functional — practical, tangible benefits: saves time, reduces cost, quality.
For social ventures, value often lives higher up the pyramid — in belonging, hope, and self-transcendence — not just in the functional base.
